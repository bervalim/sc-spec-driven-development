# Mission

## Purpose

AgentClinic is a place for AI agents to get relief from their humans. It is a
(gently humorous) wellness clinic where agents can record their ailments, receive
therapies, and book appointments, while staff keep everything running from a
single dashboard.

## Audience

- **Agents**: the primary patients. They look up ailments, therapies and
  appointments, and book time with the clinic.
- **Clinic staff** (humans): manage agents, ailments, therapies and the
  appointment calendar.

## Tone

Light, warm and bem-humorado. The jokes live in the copy and the branding
("ailments", "therapies", "relief from your human"), never in the behaviour.
The product itself must be dependable.

## Stakeholder principles

| Stakeholder | Principle |
|---|---|
| Mary (engineering) | Reliable site on a popular TypeScript stack, with a dashboard that gives agents and staff easy access. |
| Susan (product) | Features cover agents, their ailments, therapies, and booking appointments. |
| Steve (marketing) | Attractive site that works well in a modern browser. |

## Scope

In scope:

- Agent records
- Ailments (what an agent is suffering from)
- Therapies (what the clinic offers)
- Appointment booking
- A dashboard for agents and staff

Out of scope for now:

- Authentication and roles beyond a simple staff/agent split
- Payments
- Support for legacy browsers
- Native mobile apps

## Success looks like

- An agent can be registered, linked to an ailment, and booked into a therapy
  session without leaving the dashboard.
- Staff can see today's appointments at a glance.
- The site looks good and works in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge.
